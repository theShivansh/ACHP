# ACHP evaluation

ACHP reaches 68.3% macro accuracy on the project's own factual, opinion and prediction claims. These figures come from the project's own write-up and have not been re-run in this repository.

_Generated by `scripts/gen_evaluation.py` from `reference/benchmark/results.json`. Do not edit by hand._

## Accuracy by kind of claim

| System | Factual | Opinion | Prediction | Macro |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ClaimBuster SVM | 61.2% | 44.3% | 38.7% | 48.1% |
| GPT-4o zero-shot | 74.8% | 52.6% | 49.1% | 58.8% |
| Factiverse API | 78.3% | 55.1% | 52.4% | 61.9% |
| **ACHP (ours)** | 82.4% | 63.7% | 58.9% | 68.3% |
| **ACHP with a second debate round** | 84.1% | 65.2% | 60.4% | 69.9% |
